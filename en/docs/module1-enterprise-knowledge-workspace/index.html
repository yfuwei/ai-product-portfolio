Project 01 | Business AI Assistant

Finding knowledge is only the beginning.Trustworthy use is the product.

A local knowledge product that keeps redrawing its boundariesas RAG, Agent, and Wiki capabilities mature.

This project began as a local knowledge assistant used over time. As general-purpose RAG, Agent, and Wiki products matured, I stopped adding features by default and repeatedly reassessed what mature products should own, what must remain raw evidence, and what the product still needs to control.

General-purpose capabilities can be delegated to mature products,but raw evidence, usage eligibility, and final accountability must stay in the product flow.

A01 | Product Evolution

This is not a feature-upgrade roadmap

Viewed only as features, the story looks like V1 added RAG, V2 added a Router, V3 added usage eligibility, and the current stage considers LLM Wiki. What actually changed at each stage was what the product should build, what it should stop building, and where human accountability must remain.

Misleading reading

Treat each version as an increasingly complex feature list

That framing does not explain why each version made sense at the time or why the next stage did not invalidate the previous one.

Actual throughline

Real tasks × external supply × local constraints

Identify the unresolved gap, redraw Build / Use / Integrate, close the smallest viable loop, and reassess when conditions change.

A06 | The Product Manager's Role

What is the product manager responsible for?

The role is not to put every capability into the product, but to keep turning abstract judgments into concrete choices.

01

Start from real tasks

The starting point was fragmented material, hard-to-recover context, source-based preparation, and private data that should not be uploaded by default.

02

Include external supply in the decision

Continuously assess what the market has solved, what is standardized, what can be used directly, and which responsibilities cannot be delegated with the capability.

03

Turn judgment into product choices

Separate mature capabilities, custom-built components, and responsibilities that must remain, then decide what to use, integrate, or keep in the product.

04

Actively narrow product scope

Do not add larger models, multiple Agents, automatic write-back, or enterprise governance for show. Build only the smallest loop required by real tasks.

05

Define AI and human responsibilities

AI retrieves and organizes information and proposes candidate judgments. People confirm sources, applicability, risk, commitments, and final use.

06

Turn failures into product rules

Convert source filtering, path refresh, no-result handling, low-confidence clarification, version state, human confirmation, and raw-evidence review into explicit product rules.

A07 | Current Boundary and Stop Conditions

Why does the project stop here?

The core task is covered, evidence can be reviewed, and invocation, admission, and human confirmation now form a closed loop, so no more features are added.

Further expansion would return to feature accumulation rather than solve a new real problem.

Current scope

Individual knowledge tasks grounded in local material

  • Local knowledge management, material retrieval, and context recovery
  • Portfolio and interview preparation, and cross-document comparison
  • Source-backed project reviews and evidence preparation
  • Ongoing organization of high-value knowledge
Not expanding into

Enterprise capabilities without sustained demand

  • A production multi-user enterprise product
  • Enterprise permissions and full knowledge governance
  • An autonomous Agent or commercial SaaS
  • A mature product validated at scale
Core task covered→ Raw evidence reviewable→ Invocation, admission, and confirmation closed→ No new sustained demand→ Pause expansion

A08 | When to Reopen the Boundary

When should the boundary be reopened?

Reopen only the affected product decision when a new need is stable, sustained, verifiable, and clearly exceeds the current boundary.

Condition 01

New material exceeds current evidence coverage

Reassess the evidence channel only when new material types, sensitivity boundaries, or source-review requirements exceed the local evidence layer.

Condition 02

Multi-user collaboration becomes sustained demand

Open the collaboration boundary only when real tasks consistently require sharing, permissions, review, responsibility splits, and organization-level usage records.

Condition 03

Eligibility becomes a stable rule set

Expand eligibility judgment only when version, applicability, source priority, conflict, and risk form stable patterns that can support prediction, scoring, and routing.

Condition 04

Repeatable actions become authorizable

Open the execution boundary only when write, sync, publish, or business-system calls become stable needs and permissions, confirmation points, and risk limits are clear.

Condition 05

Local capability becomes a clear bottleneck

Reassess the technical substrate only when model capability, context length, complex routing, or latency persistently blocks real tasks and a new local capability clearly removes that bottleneck.

Only a new real task that clearly exceeds the current product boundary justifies another build cycle.

B02 | User Tasks and Product Scope

The user is completing a reviewable knowledge task, not a one-off Q&A

Recover rules and context, prepare source-backed material, and keep the index consistent as files change.

2.1 Real starting point

The starting point was a recurring local knowledge task: remembering a conclusion without being able to return quickly to the full evidence scattered across files. The product first solves retrieval, recovery, and source review, then abstracts the reusable evidence chain and usage boundary for enterprise knowledge work.

  • Remember a point or judgment, but not the file name;
  • Project context, interim judgments, and validation evidence are spread across files; no single document restores the full context;
  • Use a model to organize material, while preserving sources;
  • Search local files beyond Obsidian;
  • Files keep changing, so stale index entries cannot coexist with new content;
  • Private material should not be uploaded by default.

2.2 Typical tasks

The task does not end with an answer. It ends with evidence that supports preparation, comparison, and the next action.

  • Recover policies, rules, or historical judgments;
  • Restore a project's complete context;
  • Search local files outside Obsidian;
  • Compare differences and conflicts across sources;
  • Prepare a portfolio, interview material, or project review from sources;
  • Refresh the index and remove stale content after files change;
  • Carry evidence into the next preparation step.

2.3 Product goals

Find it: Retrieve relevant content across sources.

Understand it: Restore titles, sections, context, and relationships.

Review it: Return to file paths, source passages, and origins.

2.4 Product non-goals

Build scope

  • Do not build a complete knowledge platform from day one
  • Do not attempt to support every file format

Data boundary

  • Do not upload private material by default
  • Do not modify source material automatically

Product form

  • Do not make a chat box the product core
  • Do not use Agent or tool count as proof of maturity
